About the Platform

Students Voice is a web-based feedback platform designed to provide students with an accessible way to evaluate and share their experiences regarding their teachers' teaching strategies. The platform aims to encourage constructive feedback that may help teachers identify their strengths and areas for improvement. Through students' opinions, teachers may gain insights into their classroom practices and learning experiences.
